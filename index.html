Frequently Asked Questions

The TMGM Hybrid Program is designed for Introducing Brokers (IBs) and affiliates who wish to combine features of both CPA and rebate models. The program offers three flexible commission structures (20/80, 50/50, or 80/20), allowing partners to select an option that aligns with their business model and promotional approach.

The program is open to approved partners such as Introducing Brokers seeking a blended commission model, strategy providers who wish to commercialise trading activities, financial website operators with relevant traffic, and community leaders who manage active trading networks.

Partners select a preferred hybrid tier to determine their commission structure.

Example: Partner A chooses the 80/20 tier. They refer a new client from Germany (Tier 1) who opens an Edge Account, deposits $500, and trades 2 lots of FX pairs.

CPA payout: $480
Rebates payout: $1.60 per lot × 2 lots = $3.20
Total hybrid payout: $483.20

*Please note: Payouts are subject to program terms and conditions/IB agreement, and may vary depending on the instruments traded and client activity.

To participate, register as a TMGM partner and contact your account manager to complete the enrollment process.

Your dedicated account manager can provide guidance on selecting a commission structure that aligns with your portfolio, traffic sources, and business objectives.
